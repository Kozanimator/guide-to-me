About Me

I am an artist, visual effects supervisor, and digital media student. I bring imaginary things to life. If reality looks boring, I try to improve it.

My Origin Story

I started with drawing and animation, then moved into visual effects and research. One day, I asked, "What if a painting could move?" Now I keep inventing projects that refuse to stay inside their frames.

My Skills

  • Visual effects: making impossible things look real.
  • Problem solving: finding another way when technology refuses to cooperate.

I also enjoy building worlds with Unreal Engine.
