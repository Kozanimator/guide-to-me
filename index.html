About Me

I am a creative director, visual effects supervisor, and digital media master's student. I bring imaginary things to life. I try to bring what's in your mind alive.

My Origin Story

I started with drawing and animation, then moved into visual effects and academic research. One day, I asked, "What if a painting could move?" Now I keep inventing projects that refuse to stay inside their frames.

My Skills

  • Animation: making characters and objects move.
  • Visual effects: making impossible things look real in film and broadcast.
  • Problem solving: finding another way when technology refuses to cooperate.

I also enjoy building worlds with Unreal Engine.
